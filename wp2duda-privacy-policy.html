WP2Duda Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the WP2Duda Windows application

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WP2Duda handles information when you use the WP2Duda Windows desktop application.

WP2Duda is developed by VS Creation and is designed to help users migrate WordPress blog content to Duda.

We respect your privacy and do not operate WP2Duda as an advertising, analytics, or user-tracking service.

2. Information WP2Duda Accesses

WP2Duda requires information provided by the user in order to perform its migration functions.

This may include:

  • The WordPress website address entered by the user.
  • WordPress blog posts and related content retrieved from the specified website.
  • Post titles, text, headings, lists, tables, FAQs, images, links, and other publicly accessible blog content.
  • Information contained within the WordPress content being migrated.
WP2Duda does not determine whether WordPress content contains personal information. If the source website contains personal information, that information may be processed as part of the migration requested by the user.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by WP2Duda is used solely to provide the application's migration functionality.

This includes:

  • Connecting to the WordPress website specified by the user.
  • Retrieving WordPress blog content.
  • Parsing and structuring blog content.
  • Preparing content for import into Duda.
  • Generating migration/export files and data required by the workflow.
  • Temporarily making migration content available for the Duda import process when required.

4. Local Processing and Storage

WP2Duda is a Windows desktop application and performs substantial processing locally on the user's computer.

Exported files and application data may be stored locally on the user's computer in locations used by WP2Duda.

VS Creation does not operate a central WP2Duda database containing users' WordPress content.

5. WordPress Websites

WP2Duda connects to WordPress websites selected by the user. Information retrieved from those websites is processed to perform the requested migration.

WP2Duda does not control the privacy practices, security, or content of the WordPress websites that users choose to connect to. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of the websites they access.

6. Duda Import and Cloudflare Quick Tunnel

WP2Duda can use a temporary local HTTP server together with Cloudflare Quick Tunnel functionality to make migration data temporarily accessible to Duda during the import process.

When this functionality is used, data transmitted through the temporary tunnel may pass through Cloudflare's infrastructure.

Cloudflare is a separate service operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Its handling of information is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the applicable Cloudflare privacy information before using the Quick Tunnel functionality.

7. Data Collection and Analytics

WP2Duda does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or analytics purposes.

WP2Duda does not include an advertising network or user-tracking system operated by VS Creation.

8. Sharing of Information

VS Creation does not sell, rent, or trade users' information.

Information may nevertheless be transmitted to third-party services when necessary for the migration workflow initiated by the user, including the WordPress website being accessed and, when used, Cloudflare infrastructure for the temporary migration tunnel.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate rights and permissions to access and migrate the content they process using WP2Duda.

9. Data Security

WP2Duda is designed to process migration data locally where practical. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should use secure WordPress connections (HTTPS) and take appropriate precautions when handling sensitive content.

10. Children's Privacy

WP2Duda is a general-purpose desktop migration and developer utility. It is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

11. Third-Party Services

WP2Duda may interact with third-party services as necessary to perform its functionality.

These may include:

  • WordPress websites selected by the user.
  • Duda, for the destination blog migration workflow.
  • Cloudflare Quick Tunnel, when the user chooses to use the temporary tunnel functionality.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. VS Creation is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to WP2Duda, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
